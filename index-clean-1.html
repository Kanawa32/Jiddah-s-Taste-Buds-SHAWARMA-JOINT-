Our Story

Rooted In Flavour,Grilled With Passion

Jiddah's Taste Buds started with one grill, one recipe, and a simple promise — every wrap leaves our hands as good as the one before it. What began as a small corner setup has grown into Kano's go-to spot for shawarma that actually tastes like it was made for you.

We marinate our meat overnight, grill it fresh over open flame, and wrap it in warm flatbread with a sauce recipe that never leaves the kitchen. No shortcuts, no reheats — just honest, hot food.
